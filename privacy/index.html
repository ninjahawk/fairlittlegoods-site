Privacy policy

Effective TODO-DATE

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or ad trackers, and loads no third-party fonts or scripts.

What we collect

When you buy something, checkout happens on Stripe. Stripe collects your name, email, shipping address and payment details, and shares with us what we need to fill your order: your name, email, shipping address and what you bought (including the colour and size you picked). We never receive your full card number.

If you reply to an order email, we keep that message so we can answer it.

How we use it

Only to fill and ship your order, answer you, handle refunds, and keep the records the law requires. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't send marketing email unless you ask us to.

Who else sees it

Stripe (payments; see Stripe's privacy policy), our fulfilment and shipping partners (your name, address and what you bought, only to deliver it), and the carrier (your address, to deliver the package). GitHub Pages hosts this site and may log basic request data such as IP addresses for security.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, reply to your order confirmation email. We'll answer within 30 days.
