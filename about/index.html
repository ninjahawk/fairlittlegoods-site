About

Fair Little Goods is a small shop of original designs on tees, hoodies, mugs, totes and art prints.

Every design started as a drawing for this shop, which is why you won't see them anywhere else. We keep the jokes gentle, the colours warm and the lines hand-drawn.

What you get

Tees are 4.2 oz combed, ring-spun cotton with the print set into the fabric, so it stays soft. Hoodies are 8 oz cotton-polyester fleece. Mugs are glossy ceramic and go in the dishwasher. Art prints are on thick matte paper. Each product page lists the real measurements, so you know the fit before you buy.

How we work

We're a small, independent shop. Checkout runs on Stripe, shipping inside the US is free and tracked, and if anything arrives damaged, misprinted or wrong, we replace it or refund it in full, with nothing to send back. New designs go up most weeks.
