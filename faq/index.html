Frequently asked questions

How do the tees and hoodies fit?

They're a unisex fit and true to size. Each product page has a size chart: lay a tee or hoodie you like flat, measure straight across the chest, and pick the closest size.

How do I wash them?

Turn tees and hoodies inside out, wash cold, and tumble dry low or hang to dry. Don't iron over the print. Mugs can go in the dishwasher and microwave.

How big are the stickers?

Stickers on the sheet are about 1.9 inches each, and the sheet is 5.8 x 8.3 inches. They're weather-resistant matte vinyl, so rain and the odd spill are fine.

How long until my order arrives?

Orders arrive in about 5 to 12 business days in the US. See Shipping.

Where do you ship?

Right now we ship to the United States, for free.

How do I pay?

Checkout is handled by Stripe, which takes cards and common wallets. We never see or store your card number.

Can I return something that doesn't fit?

Because every piece is printed just for you, we can't take returns for a change of mind or the wrong size. Check the size chart first, and if you're unsure, email us before you order and we'll help you pick.
